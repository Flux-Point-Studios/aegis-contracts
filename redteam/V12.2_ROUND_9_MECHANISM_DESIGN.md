## Findings

### R9-MECH-1 — Partner uncompensated on Cancel for premiums < 10 000 ADA

- **Severity:** INFO. Not exploit; documented surface for the partner
  contract narrative.
- **Spec section / file:line:** V12.2 spec §3.6 (lines 1601-1630) and
  cancel-path silent absorb at `validators/pool.ak:1295-1306`. Numeric
  identity at `lib/aegis/pricing.ak:74-80, 152-166`.
- **Description.** Cancel-path Hybrid fee operates on
  `cancellation_fee = 10% × premium`, so `raw_fee_cancel = 0.002 ×
  premium`. At 2000 bps partner cap, threshold where partner ACTUALLY
  receives a cancel-side cut is `premium ≥ 10 000 ADA` (50× higher than
  the Underwrite-side 500 ADA threshold). Below that, partner is silently
  absorbed on cancel despite the datum recording 20% share.
- **+EV per cycle.** Zero — spec consequence (§3.6), not exploit.
- **Remediation.** Off-chain only: surface cancel-side absorb threshold
  in partner-registry dashboard. Add §10.3a to spec.

### R9-MECH-2 — RemoveLiquidity front-run on imminent Claim

- **Severity:** MEDIUM. Adverse selection that LPs systematically eat
  when oracle flips ITM with any detectable lag. Bounded by pool
  `available_liquidity` (= TVL − active_coverage).
- **Spec section / file:line:** `validators/pool.ak:925-972`
  (RemoveLiquidity arm), `lib/aegis/pool.ak:78-84` (`can_withdraw`
  enforces only `total - withdrawal >= active_coverage`). V12.2 spec
  has NO treatment of this surface.
- **Description.** Aegis Claims are user-initiated. When the oracle
  prints ITM (price ≤ strike), an insured can wait an arbitrary
  fresh-window duration (49-70 min depending on provider) before
  submitting Claim. During that window: (a) on-chain `total_liquidity`
  still reflects pre-payout NAV; (b) `RemoveLiquidity` does NOT check
  for live ITM-eligible policies.

  An LP who watches oracle UTxOs can detect ITM events before the insured
  submits Claim. They race-submit `RemoveLiquidity` against the same
  canonical pool UTxO. eUTxO-deterministic: either RemoveLiquidity wins
  (LP exits at pre-Claim NAV) or Claim wins (LP retries).

  **Why stronger than normal adverse selection.** The LP gets PERFECT
  signal (oracle UTxO is a public chain artefact, ITM is a single integer
  comparison). No market-making cost, no commit-reveal, no slashing.
  Rational LPs WILL drain available_liquidity at every ITM flip — leaving
  un-watchful LPs holding the full payout.

- **Exploit construction.** Pool: 10 000 ADA TVL, 9 000 ADA
  active_coverage (90 policies × 100 ADA coverage each). LP-A holds 50%
  of lp_supply. ADA strike = $0.20. Oracle prints $0.18 at slot S0.
  Freshness window = 70 min.

  At slot S0+5s, LP-A submits `RemoveLiquidity { amount: 1_000_000_000 }`
  (= 1 000 ADA = all available_liquidity). Validator's `can_withdraw`:
  `10 000 − 1 000 = 9 000 >= 9 000` → PASS. lp_supply decrements by
  `1 000 × old_supply / 10 000 = 0.1 × old_supply`. LP-A pulls out
  10% of supply for 1 000 ADA cash. Their remaining holding is `(0.5 −
  0.1) / 0.9 = 0.444` of post-event lp_supply.

  Insureds Claim through S0+70min. Pool drops to `10 000 − 1 000 − 9 000 =
  0 ADA`. Lp_supply unchanged on Claim path.

  Final position: LP-A holds 1 000 ADA cash + 0.444 × 0 = 0 in pool =
  **1 000 ADA total**. LP-B (held the other 50% of pre-event supply,
  didn't withdraw) holds `0.5 / 0.9 × 0 = 0` ADA. **LP-B lost their full
  5 000-ADA pre-event NAV; LP-A captured 1 000 ADA of it.**

  The pool's pre-Claim 1 000 ADA buffer (= `available_liquidity`) was
  redistributed from LP-B-share to LP-A by the race. Insureds got the
  full 9 000 ADA they were promised.

- **+EV per cycle for LP-A:** up to `available_liquidity` × `LP-A's_share`.
  At 80% pool utilization typical for mature pools, `available_liquidity
  = 0.2 × TVL`. At 100 000 ADA mainnet TVL, that's 20 000 ADA captured per
  oracle flip if LP-A holds 100% of `available_liquidity`'s claim.

- **Why R7/R8 missed it.** V12.2 spec is silent on withdrawal-vs-pending-
  claim race. R8 focused on same-tx exploits. This is a cross-tx adverse-
  selection issue.

- **Remediation.** Two options:
  1. **Timelocked RemoveLiquidity.** 24-h delay between request and
     execution, snapshot LP balance at request. Standard insurance-pool
     architecture (Nexus Mutual). Requires new datum field.
  2. **Withdrawal fee = expected-loss-rate.** Charge a fee proportional
     to oracle-side risk. Adds oracle dependency to RemoveLiquidity.

  Option 1 is canonical. Documenting as a v12.3 roadmap item is
  minimum-viable.

### R9-MECH-3 — Partner share economics jump at 100 ADA boundary

- **Severity:** LOW. Documentation gap; misaligned-incentive surface.
- **Spec section / file:line:** `lib/aegis/pricing.ak:74-80`
  (`calculate_fee_total`) and split at `:152-166`. Boundary where
  `premium × fee_bps / 10_000 == min_utxo` (= 100 ADA at default config).
- **Description.** For premiums below 100 ADA, `fee_total = 2 ADA`
  (floor). For premiums at/above 100 ADA, `fee_total = premium × 2%`
  (linear). At 20% partner cap:
  - premium = 400 ADA: fee_total = 8, partner_cut = 1.6 < floor → absorb,
    partner gets 0.
  - premium = 500 ADA: fee_total = 10, partner_cut = 2 → paid.

  Partner's effective rate per ADA of premium goes from 0% (premium ≤
  499 ADA) to 0.4% (premium = 500 ADA) DISCONTINUOUSLY.
- **Exploit construction.** A frontend integrator collecting partner
  fees has a strict incentive to upsell users from 400-ADA to 500-ADA
  premium at the same coverage. Net flows: user −100, partner +2, team
  +6 (fee_total 2→10), pool +88. Misaligned-incentive surface; user
  bears the cost.
- **+EV per cycle.** Partner: +2 ADA above boundary, 0 below. User:
  −100 ADA upsell tax.
- **Remediation.** None on-chain (discontinuity is intended Hybrid fee
  design). Off-chain: surface boundary in partner-registry UI so
  integrators don't push users into suboptimal premium tiers.

### R9-MECH-4 — Stale-oracle Claim window (AegisSelf publisher pause)

- **Severity:** LOW. Bounded by `valid_until` (typically 70 min). Real
  ITM event required.
- **Spec section / file:line:** `validators/policy.ak:199` (Claim
  freshness gate: `tx_lower <= price.valid_until`),
  `lib/aegis/oracle/aegis_self.ak:114-123` (valid_until publisher-set,
  typically `created_at + 70 min`).
- **Description.** AegisSelf publisher writes a per-publish
  `valid_until` into the datum. If publisher pauses (NSSM service down,
  ops failure), the LAST published price stays canonical until
  `valid_until` elapses. If the price WAS ITM at publish but recovered
  off-chain and then crashed again, a user can submit Claim against the
  stale ITM print AFTER the recovery — banking payout while the market
  thinks they're OTM.

  Live observation: `/api/oracle/price` returns `fresh: false` for all
  four tested AegisSelf assets, confirming the publisher-pause condition
  exists on preprod today.

- **Exploit construction.** At slot S0, publisher writes ADA = $0.20,
  strike = $0.21, `valid_until = S0 + 70 min`. Off-chain ADA recovers to
  $0.25 by S0+30min. User submits Claim at S0+60min against stale $0.20
  reading. Payout = coverage_amount (e.g., 100 ADA). Profit = coverage −
  premium = 100 − 2 = 98 ADA at min_premium config. Validator accepts:
  `tx_lower >= price.observed_at` (PASS), `tx_lower <= price.valid_until`
  (S0+60min < S0+70min, PASS), `price.value <= strike` ($0.20 < $0.21,
  PASS).
- **+EV per cycle.** Up to coverage_amount per stale ITM print, capped
  by the 70-min freshness window.
- **Why R7/R8 missed it.** R7/R8 verified freshness gates exist; they
  didn't analyze economic exposure of the publisher's chosen
  `valid_until`. Off-chain ops concern; on-chain validator trusts
  publisher signed `valid_until`.
- **Remediation.** Three options:
  1. Tighten AegisSelf publisher `valid_until` from 70 min to 15 min.
     Off-chain change.
  2. Add validator-side ceiling `valid_until − observed_at <= 15min` so
     publisher cannot write longer freshness. 1-line validator change +
     hash rotation.
  3. Document worst-case 70-min window in user FAQ; price premiums
     accordingly.

  Option 2 is strongest but requires redeploy.

### R9-MECH-5 — Claim/Expire boundary inclusivity gap

- **Severity:** LOW. 1-ms gap, no value flows.
- **Spec section / file:line:** `validators/policy.ak:203` (Claim:
  `tx_upper <= datum.expiry_time`), `:297` (Expire: `tx_lower >
  datum.expiry_time`).
- **Description.** Claim and Expire use opposite-direction bounds
  against the same `expiry_time`. A tx whose validity range straddles
  `expiry_time` (`tx_lower <= expiry_time < tx_upper`) fails both. User
  burns ~0.5 ADA tx fee on the rejected attempt and rebuilds with a
  tighter range.
- **+EV per cycle.** Zero — no value transfer, just wasted gas.
- **Remediation.** None required. Document the gap. Optionally rewrite
  predicates to be exhaustive at the boundary — but that introduces a
  same-slot race surface that doesn't currently exist. **Recommend
  leave as-is.**

## Surfaces tested and rejected (with reason)

### S1 — Partner-share griefing via small-policy stacking
Partner only receives `partner_cut` if `partner_cut_raw >= min_utxo`.
Stacking small policies (premium < 500 ADA at 20% share) silently
absorbs every partner cut to team. Above 500 ADA, attacker is paying
245× the captured cut (490 ADA into pool to extract 2 ADA). Attacker
self-pwns catastrophically. **Reject.**

### S2 — Cancel cycles at thresholds other than R7-A
At mainnet `min_premium = 100 ADA`: Underwrite leg pool grows 98 ADA;
Cancel leg pool shrinks 92 ADA. Net per cycle: +6 ADA for LP, −11.5 ADA
for user (tx fees + treasury + lost 10%). **LP gains per cycle at
mainnet config. Reject.**

### S3 — Silent-absorb collusion (user-as-partner self-deal)
User sets `partner_address = <own wallet>`, `partner_share_bps = 2000`.
At premium = 500 ADA, user-as-partner receives 2 ADA from team's slice.
Pool growth is identical (fee_total deterministic from premium + fee_bps
+ min_utxo). The user is effectively granting themselves a 0.4% volume
discount out of TEAM revenue (not LP). R8-DRAIN-1 closes aliasing to
team/pool/policy_script but NOT to user's own address — by design per
V12.2 §10.3 finding #1 ("user receives 0 ADA — self-pwned"). At premium
≥ 500 ADA where silent absorb doesn't activate, the user DOES receive
the cut. Bounded by 20% × fee_total = 0.4% of premium. **Documented
trust model, not exploit. Reject.**

### S4 — Premium/coverage ratio +EV gaming
At 50× leverage and 2% fee, breakeven P(claim) = 0.0196 = 1.96%. Any
policy with realized strike-crossing probability > 1.96% is +EV for the
user, −EV for the LP. **This is the LP's KNOWN risk profile.** Spec
acknowledges LPs take policy risk (§10.1, §10.4c). The validator is
denomination-agnostic; pricing is the off-chain underwriter's job.
**Intended behavior. Reject.**

### S5 — Oracle-flap arbitrage
**Elevated to R9-MECH-4.**

### S6 — Treasury donation gaming
The donation is enforced as `>=` against a value derived from datum
(`pool.ak:768-778`, `:1365-1371`). Derivation uses `premium` and
constant `treasury_share_bps`. The only path to `required_donation == 0`
is `premium <= 50` lovelace, which fails `is_above_minimum(2_000_000)`.
R8 confirmed this. **No surface. Reject.**

### S7 — LP pool dilution (sandwich on AddLiquidity)
Pure sandwich on AddLiquidity harms the JOINING LP (they buy in at
pre-claim NAV, get post-claim NAV). New LPs are PROTECTED by adverse
selection — they should just not join right before claims. The dangerous
direction is the OPPOSITE: LPs EXITING. **Elevated to R9-MECH-2.**

### S8 — Concurrent Underwrite race
Cardano deterministic eUTxO ordering means at most one of N competing
Underwrites succeeds; losers' txs never include, no fee burned. The
griefing direction (attacker pre-empts honest user) costs attacker full
premium + tx fee. **No asymmetric harm. Reject.**

### S9 — Cancel/Expire timing arbitrage
**Elevated to R9-MECH-5** (1-ms inclusivity gap, no value transfer).

### S10 — Cross-asset basis arbitrage (AegisSelf vs Indigo)
User opens 100-ADA-premium "ADA crashes" on AegisSelf + 100-ADA-premium
"iADA crashes" on Indigo. Pays 200 ADA premium for max 200 ADA payout.
For dual-leg +EV, need P(both strike) materially > 0 — but real ADA
crash drags iADA down (correlated). At realistic correlations, dual-leg
is exactly priced. **No +EV. Reject.**

### S11 — Partner share tier-jumping (NEW)
**Elevated to R9-MECH-3** (100 ADA boundary economics discontinuity).

### S12 — LP front-run via RemoveLiquidity (NEW)
**Elevated to R9-MECH-2** (principal mechanism-design finding).

## Confidence on coverage

- **HIGH** on partner-share / silent-absorb / cancel-cycle math. Traced
  all four code paths (Underwrite, BatchUnderwrite, Cancel,
  AcceptCancellation) and `calculate_protocol_fee_split` end-to-end.
  R8-DRAIN-1's `partner_address_not_aliased` correctly applied at three
  fee-bearing branches.
- **MEDIUM** on R9-MECH-2 (LP front-run). Traced RemoveLiquidity / Claim
  interaction; verified `can_withdraw` blocks the most aggressive form
  (LP can't withdraw past `active_coverage`). Remaining surface is
  `available_liquidity` race — bounded but real. Multi-round LP strategy
  modeling deferred to Round 10.
- **MEDIUM** on R9-MECH-4 (oracle flap). 70-min ceiling is real; didn't
  model cross-publisher race (AegisSelf paused + Indigo refreshes
  simultaneously) — requires publisher infra modeling beyond on-chain.
- **HIGH** that the 10 brief surfaces have been considered. Five
  elevated (S5, S7-partial, S9, S11-new, S12-new); five rejected with
  citations.
- **LOWER** on adversary-chosen tx orderings under cross-protocol
  composition (Indigo CDP liquidation race vs Aegis BatchClaim). R8
  flagged this as future work; Round 9 is on-chain Aegis-only.

## Recommendation

**No mainnet blocker added by Round 9.** Net findings:

| Finding | Severity | Path forward |
|---|---|---|
| R9-MECH-1 (partner cancel uncompensated < 10k premium) | INFO | Doc only — add spec §10.3a |
| R9-MECH-2 (LP front-run via RemoveLiquidity) | MEDIUM | v12.3 — timelocked RemoveLiquidity OR exit-fee |
| R9-MECH-3 (partner tier-jumping at 100 ADA) | LOW | Doc only — surface in partner-registry UI |
| R9-MECH-4 (70-min stale oracle window) | LOW | Tighten AegisSelf `valid_until` OR add validator ceiling |
| R9-MECH-5 (Claim/Expire 1-ms gap) | LOW | Doc only |

Protocol is economically sound under V12.2 + R7 + R8 fixes for documented
use cases (small-N policies, single-publisher AegisSelf, four Indigo
iAssets, 50× leverage cap). R9-MECH-2 (LP front-run) is the only finding
warranting v12.3 work — and it's endemic to permissionless insurance
pools rather than specific to Aegis. R9-MECH-4 (oracle flap) is the only
finding with an on-chain remediation that's a small diff.

Total: **1 MEDIUM, 3 LOW, 1 INFO. Zero HIGH. Zero CRITICAL.**
